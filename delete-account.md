# Delete Your FLOWS Account

You can delete your FLOWS account and all associated data at any time.

## Option 1 — In-App Deletion (Recommended)

1. Open the **FLOWS** app on your device.
2. Tap the **Settings** icon (gear, bottom-right).
3. Open **Account → Delete Account**.
4. Confirm the action.

Your data will be permanently deleted from our systems within 30 days.

## Option 2 — Request Deletion by Email

If you cannot access the app:

1. Send an email to **flowsapp.dev@gmail.com** from the email address linked
   to your FLOWS account.
2. Use the subject line: **Account Deletion Request**.
3. We will process your request and confirm deletion within 30 days.

## What Is Deleted

- Account credentials (email, user ID)
- All budget data: tabs, categories, funds, transactions, notes
- App preferences and settings
- Cloud-synced data on our servers
- Analytics events linked to your user ID

## What Is Retained

- **Anonymized analytics aggregates** that cannot be linked back to you.
- **Subscription billing records** — retained as required by tax and consumer
  protection law (typically up to 7 years), then permanently deleted.
