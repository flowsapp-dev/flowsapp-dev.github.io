# Delete Your FLOW$ Account

You can delete your FLOW$ account at any time. Account deletion and local
data wipe are two separate actions in the app — see below.

## Delete Your Account

1. Open the **FLOW$** app on your device.
2. Tap the **Settings** icon (gear, bottom-right).
3. Open **Account → Delete Account**.
4. Tap **Remove** to confirm (or **Cancel** to abort).

This will:
- Sign you out of your current session.
- Permanently delete your account from our authentication system (Firebase).
- Permanently delete all of your cloud-synced data: budgets, transactions,
  funds, categories, tabs, and notes.

## Wipe Your Local Data

Account deletion does **not** remove the budgets, transactions, and
preferences stored on your device. To clear that data:

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Use **Reset All Data**.

This wipes all locally-stored user content. The app returns to a clean
first-launch state.

## Request Deletion by Email

If you cannot access the app (lost device, uninstalled):

1. Send an email to **flowsapp.dev@gmail.com** from the email linked to
   your FLOW$ account.
2. Subject: **Account Deletion Request**.
3. We will process your request within 30 days.

## What Is Retained

- **Subscription billing records** — retained as required by tax and
  consumer protection law (typically up to 7 years), then permanently
  deleted.
