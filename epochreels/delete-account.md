You can request deletion of your Epoch Reels player account and its data at any
time. Epoch Reels plays as a guest — your progress is tied to an anonymous player
profile, not to an email or username. Because of this, deletion is handled by
request, and clearing your local data is a separate action. See both below.

## Request Account Deletion

1. Send an email to **flowsapp.dev@gmail.com**.
2. Subject: **Epoch Reels — Account Deletion Request**.
3. To help us locate your anonymous profile, please include any of the
   following you have: your in-game **Player ID** (if shown in the app, usually
   under Settings or About), your device model, and the approximate date you
   started playing.

We will process your request within **30 days**.

This will permanently delete your player profile and all associated cloud data,
including:

- your virtual coin balance and Piggy Bank contents;
- your level, progress, missions, and bonuses;
- game preferences and settings stored on our servers.

Virtual coins have no real-world value and are not refundable or convertible to
cash.

## Wipe Your Local Data {#wipe-your-local-data}

Account deletion does **not** remove the game data cached on your device. To
clear it:

- **Android:** open **Settings → Apps → Epoch Reels → Storage → Clear data**, or
  simply **uninstall** the app.

This wipes all locally-stored game data. The app returns to a clean
first-launch state.

## What Is Retained

- **Purchase and billing records** — in-app purchases are processed by Google
  Play; we retain limited purchase records as required by tax and consumer
  protection law (typically up to 7 years), after which they are permanently
  deleted.
